Burkina Faso drought anticipatory action

Analyses and monitoring notes for the CERF-funded anticipatory-action framework for seasonal drought in Burkina Faso. The 2026 framework covers four provinces (Loroum, Oudalan, Séno, Yagha) and activates on either an April ECMWF SEAS5 rainfall forecast or a July–September JRC ASAP biomass alert.

Analyses

National bulletins vs the ASAP trigger — September 2026

After the provisional non-activation on the 3rd dekad of August, partners shared three Burkinabè documents: ANAM's July drought bulletin, ANAM's rainfall situation at 31 August, and the SAP-GTP joint field mission. How they compare with the JRC ASAP alerts over the four provinces, and whether any could serve as a trigger.
